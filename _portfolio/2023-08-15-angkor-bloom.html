Project Overview

The RoboCon 'Angkor Bloom' competition is one of the most prestigious robotics competitions for college students in China. This competition challenges teams to design and build robots capable of completing complex tasks related to the Angkor theme, requiring advanced mechanical design, control systems, and strategic planning.

From approximately December 2022 through the national finals held on July 5–8, 2023, I participated in this national-level competition as part of a team from South China University of Technology. Our team worked together to design, implement, and optimize a robotic system that could successfully complete the competition's challenging tasks.

Competition Highlights

  • National Recognition: Competed at the national level among top universities across China.
  • Team Achievement: Collaborated effectively with team members to achieve Third Prize.
  • Technical Skills: Applied knowledge in robotics, mechanical design, control systems, and embedded programming.
  • Problem Solving: Addressed complex engineering challenges under competition time constraints.

Technical Contributions

Throughout this competition project, I contributed to various aspects of the robot development process:

  • Mechanical design and fabrication of robot components
  • Control system development and implementation
  • Testing and optimization of robot performance
  • Strategic planning and competition execution

Awards & Recognition

Third Prize in the National College Student Robotics Competition "Angkor Bloom," 2023

This achievement represents recognition of our team's technical skills, collaboration, and dedication to robotics engineering. The competition provided valuable experience in real-world robot development, competition strategy, and team collaboration.
